Que - What is “occupancy” in CUDA?

Ans - Occupancy = Active warps per SM / Maximum warps per SM. 

Higher Occupancy( >= 70%) is good. 
Lower Occupancy means GPU is idle. 
100% Occupancy != peak performance.

Compute bound kernels will do arithematic most of the time and will not be waiting for memory again and again. 
And compute bound kernel will require more registers/thread - this hits the register limit - so fewer warps per second. 
So the Occupancy could be less for compute bound kernels. Which is not bad by the way. This is how it is.
