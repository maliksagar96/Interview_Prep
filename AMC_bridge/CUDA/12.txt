Que - How do you measure CUDA application performance? What could hit the performance? or What are the bottlenecks? 

Ans - 1) Uncoalesced memory leads to low bandwidth utilisation < 50%. 
      2) Insufficient occupancy - High "no instructions" stalls + low active warps/SM.
      3) Warp divergence - Minimize if/else with mixed warp outcomes; reorganize data.
      4) Shared memory bank conflicts - Low shared memory throughput despite heavy usage, avoid strides. 
      5) Scattered writes - Write bandwidth << read bandwidth.
