12. Compute the distance between two arbitrary infinite lines in 3D.

Case 1
If the lines are parallel i.e. d1 x d2 = 0
Then what we want is D = |(p2-p1)xd1| / |d1|

We can look at the cross product of (only magnitude) |(p1-p2)xd1| = |d1| x component of (p1-p2) perpendicular to d1. 

Case 2
Non parallel lines.
then the closest distance will be along the direction which is perpendicular to both the line.

so D = |(p2 - p1).(d1 x d2)| / |d1 x d2|,

This is the scalar triple product. If we look carefull there is a paralleopiped here in the play. We divide the volume of the parallelopiped by the area of base parallelogram. This gives height. 

Wonderful way to visualise.
