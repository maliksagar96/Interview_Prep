7. How many parameters are required to uniquely define a sphere/cone/cylinder/torus? What are the parameters?

Ans - 1) Sphere needs 4. Radius and center x, y and z. 
2) Cone -   a) Axis direction
            b) Height
            c) Aperture angle
            d) Apex vertiex point

3) Cylinder a) Axis direction   
            b) Height
            c) Base Radius
            d) Base Center

4) Torus    a) Center.   
            b) Axis.
            c) Radius from the center.
            d) Radius of the circle that will revolve around this radius.
