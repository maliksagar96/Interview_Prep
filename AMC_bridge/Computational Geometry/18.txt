8. Is it possible to find a circle's radius and the center point from three points lying on the circle? If yes, how would you do so? Is it possible to do with less than 3 points? 
Ans. To find the circumcircle we find the perpendicular bisector of the sides of the triangle and from this we can find radius
by joining the circumcenter and any vertex of the triangle. This is the geometrical way. 

This can be done algebracially as well. 

(x - x1)^2 + (y - y1)^2 = (x - x2)^2 + (y - y2)^2
(x - x2)^2 + (y - y2)^2 = (x - x3)^2 + (y - y3)^2

This will give us 2 equations in 2 variables. And we can solve this.
